What is Kalug?

Kalug is a collaborative, cant, and logical language made because me and my older cousin used to speak gibberish and claimed it was a secret langusge.

Why is Kalug:

  • Logical language because it combines multiple people's ideas on what gibberish is or isn't
  • Cant language because my cousin and i said gibberish was a secret language so i'm making it a cant
  • Collaborative language because more than 1 people contributed to the lexicon (mostly me, Ambreg_Langs, and FedeConlangerITA)
